## Content judgments

- No invented customer logos, testimonials, revenue, installed base, certifications or clinical performance results.
- The presentation lists Cancer but contains no dedicated cancer specification. Its page retains the shared clinical and confirmatory-referral copy; no cancer performance figures or dedicated model specification are invented.
- Original clinical workflow wording, including “pre-draft reports in milliseconds”, is retained as requested; this audit checks correspondence with the presentation, not clinical validation.
- The febrile illustration contains possible findings, not diagnostic proof of infection. The website describes imaging as supportive of clinical and confirmatory assessment.
- The source dashboard contains conflicting attention values between overlaid slide text and embedded artwork. The interactive synthetic example uses the explicit overlaid values (300 critical, 400 pending, 100 follow-ups due, 200 delayed referrals). The original graphic is preserved separately as a labelled concept.
- Programme/district/date filters and CSV exports operate on 12,460 synthetic records. Figures are demonstrations, not reported customer outcomes.
- No contact address or clinical sign-in destination was supplied. The existing honest downloadable technical briefing is retained; no enquiry is falsely shown as sent and no clinical-login endpoint is invented.

## Visual and technical delivery

- 7 newly generated high-resolution assets, optimised to WebP.
- Presentation-derived assets include the complete six workflow diagrams and maternal/febrile workflow artwork, alongside original care and organ-module illustrations.
- Native image dimensions, responsive image containers, lazy loading of noncritical images, keyboard tabs, mobile navigation and native dialog focus management.
- All 13 page entrypoints, links, anchors, image files, JavaScript syntax and synthetic-data counts checked. Browser QA was not requested and was not performed.

## September 2026 alignment audit

All 22 slides were reviewed, including text embedded in their raster artwork. Fixed missing care-stage wording and illustrations, module descriptions, maternal five-step sequence, febrile four-step sequence, clinical benefits, ICU example findings, facility filtering, Impact navigation, and original workflow diagrams. Original tagline, company copy and final CTA remain unchanged.

Source conflicts and operational exclusions:
- Slide 7 pairs a thoracic heading and thoracic native text with febrile-disease artwork. Thoracic wording stays on the thoracic page; the full febrile artwork and its wording appear on the infectious-disease page.
- Slide 5 overlay values differ from embedded artwork. The demonstration follows overlay values; the source dashboard image remains visible and labelled illustrative.
- Sign In appears in deck navigation, but no authentication destination is supplied. It is not fabricated. Request Demo prepares a downloadable brief; delivery requires a real contact endpoint. Neither is a working clinical service.
- Cancer is listed on slide 6 without a dedicated clinical specification.
- Example case findings and programme counts are illustrations, not patient records or verified product outcomes.

Validation: 13 generated pages, local links/assets/anchors, JavaScript syntax, restored content and facility-filter behavior checked. No browser visual QA performed in this audit.
